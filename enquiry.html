Get Involved

Volunteer your time, sponsor a garden, or partner with us — together we can end food insecurity in Gauteng.

Make an impact

Ways to Get Involved

Volunteer

Spend a morning in the garden, assist with workshops, or bring your professional skills. Every hour makes a real difference to families in our communities.

  • Weekday and Saturday sessions at gardens in four townships
  • Induction and training provided
  • No gardening experience needed
Volunteer with us

Sponsor

Corporate and individual sponsorships fund garden equipment, seeds, and educational materials. Your support is recognised on our website and at garden sites.

  • Sponsor a garden from R5,000 per year
  • Equipment packs from R1,500
  • Workshop sponsorship from R2,500
Become a sponsor

Partner

Partner with us through CSI initiatives, employee volunteering days, or in-kind donations — and earn a social impact report for your stakeholders.

  • Employee volunteering days for teams
  • CSI programme partnerships
  • In-kind donations of tools, seeds and materials
