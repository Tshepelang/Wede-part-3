Our Story

From a single garden in Soweto to a network of twelve — growing food security and community across Gauteng.

How we began

Rooted in Community

Roots of Hope Foundation was founded in 2019 by community activist Nandi Dlamini in direct response to rising food insecurity affecting low-income families across Gauteng. Witnessing families in Soweto struggle to access fresh, nutritious food, Nandi transformed a vacant plot into the organisation's first community vegetable garden.

What began as a single garden with twelve volunteer families has since grown into a network of twelve gardens across four townships — Soweto, Alexandra, Tembisa, and Katlehong. Today, Roots of Hope serves over 3,000 beneficiaries annually, combining sustainable urban farming with nutrition education to create lasting change.

The organisation is registered as a non-profit in accordance with the South African Non-Profit Organisations Act 71 of 1997, supported by a growing network of corporate sponsors, individual donors, and community volunteers.

What drives us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower South African communities through sustainable urban farming, nutrition education, and food security programmes that create lasting change.

Our Vision

A food-secure South Africa where every community has access to fresh, nutritious food and the knowledge to cultivate it.
