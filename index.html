Who we are

Fighting Food Insecurity
from the Ground Up

Founded in 2019 by community activist Nandi Dlamini, Roots of Hope Foundation began as a single vegetable garden in Soweto. Today we operate a network of twelve gardens across Soweto, Alexandra, Tembisa, and Katlehong — serving thousands of families with fresh produce, skills training, and a stronger sense of community.

Our mission: to empower South African communities through sustainable urban farming, nutrition education, and food security programmes that create lasting change.
