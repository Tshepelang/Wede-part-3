Our Programmes

From community vegetable gardens to youth leadership — discover how Roots of Hope is creating lasting change across Gauteng.

Programme 1

Urban Food Gardens

Our flagship programme operates twelve community vegetable gardens across Soweto, Alexandra, Tembisa, and Katlehong. Each garden is managed by trained community members and produces a diverse range of vegetables — including spinach, tomatoes, butternut, and sweet potato — available to beneficiary families throughout the growing season.

Garden participants receive:

  • Hands-on training in sustainable urban farming techniques
  • Regular access to fresh, organically grown produce
  • Seeds, tools, and equipment provided by the foundation
  • Monthly visits from agricultural advisors

Programme 2

Nutrition Education

Every week, Roots of Hope facilitates nutrition education workshops at garden sites and community centres across all four townships. Led by trained health workers and dietitians, these sessions give families the knowledge they need to make informed choices about their diet.

Workshop topics include:

  • Understanding food groups and balanced meals
  • Budget-friendly cooking with fresh garden produce
  • Food preservation and seasonal eating
  • Nutrition for children aged 0–5 and pregnant mothers

Programme 3

Youth Development

The Youth Development Programme engages young people aged 12–25 in sustainable agriculture, environmental education, and community leadership. Participants complete a structured 12-week curriculum combining practical garden work with classroom-based learning in entrepreneurship and environmental stewardship.

Programme outcomes include:

  • Certificate in Urban Farming and Food Security (NQF-aligned)
  • Leadership and project management skills
  • Community service hours recognised by partner schools
  • Mentorship from experienced agricultural professionals
